## Product Requirements Document: Tiba Living Corporate Portfolio

### 1. Project Overview & Objectives

* **Goal:** Create an immersive, online portfolio for Tiba Living, a boutique real estate development company defined by an authentic approach to design, development, and delivery^^. The site must attract investors and buyers by showcasing the company's global portfolio and core philosophies.
* **Design Ethos:** Minimalist, timeless, and highly interactive. The site will utilize large-format media, elegant typography, and seamless micro-interactions inspired by Aman resorts.
* **Brand Positioning:** Tiba means "to arrive"^^. **The brand focuses on quiet luxury, selecting untouched sanctuaries to create destinations that build community and ignite the spirit**^^.
  **+1**

---

### 2. Visual Direction & UI Constraints

* **Color Usage:** The brand color palette will be used  **sparingly** . The UI will rely heavily on the Ivory background to create negative space, with Stone used for typography to ensure stark, high-contrast minimalism. Sunset, River Green, Dusk Blue, and Moss will be reserved strictly for subtle hover states, micro-interactions, or artistic background gradients in the "Philosophy" section.
* **Typography:** Baskerville (Serif) for elegant, editorial headings; Montserrat (Sans-Serif) for clean, highly legible body copy and UI elements.
* **Logomark:** The Tiba temple logomark (incorporating the mountain, temple gate, ocean ripples, steps, and sun) will be used as a subtle, recurring motif—such as a watermark on image transitions or a loading animation.

---

### 3. Information Architecture (Sitemap)

The navigation will be hidden behind a minimalist "Hamburger" menu, opening into a full-screen overlay.

1. **Home** (Immersive reel of projects and high-level introduction)
2. **Our DNA / Philosophy** (An artistic deep-dive into development principles)
3. **Portfolio** (The visual index of all developments)
   * Individual Project Pages (8 total)
4. **About** (Founders and company background)
5. **Inquire / Contact** (For investors and buyers)

---

### 4. Page-by-Page Content & Layout Requirements

#### A. Home Page

* **Hero Section:** A full-bleed, cinematic auto-playing video reel showcasing the diverse locations (Tennessee, Bali, California). **Minimal text overlay: "TIBA LIVING" and "Creating destinations"**^^.
* **The Mission Block:** A stark, text-only editorial block using Baskerville. **Copy focus: "At TIBA living, we create more than just buildings. We craft moments, curate experiences, and design the backdrop of your new life"**^^.
* **Featured Developments Carousel:** An Aman-style, smooth horizontal scrolling carousel highlighting 3-4 flagship projects (e.g., House SJ, Tiba Hills, Tiba Tennessee) with large imagery and subtle hover effects that reveal the project name.

#### B. Our DNA (Philosophy Page)

This page must be highly artistic, using scroll-triggered animations to reveal each pillar as the user moves down the page.

* **Natural Assimilation:** Text explaining the focus on working with mesmerising landscapes to create an extension of beauty^^.
* **Sustainable Development:** Highlight the partnership with Ecocrete and focus on longevity, footprint, and climate^^.
* **Climate Adaptability:** Detail the practices adapted specifically to the unique climate of Bali^^.
* **Timeless Development:** Emphasize intentional design and construction^^.
* **Informed Modernity:** Explain the appreciation for the socio-economic environment while capturing the true essence of Bali^^.

#### C. Portfolio Overview

* **Layout:** A masonry or asymmetrical grid of large, high-resolution images. No bounding boxes or heavy borders. Project titles appear subtly on hover.

### 5. Technical & Interactive Requirements

To achieve the "Aman-level" digital experience:

* **Progressive Image Loading:** Implement blurred image placeholders that smoothly transition to high-resolution as the user scrolls.
* **Parallax & Scroll Triggers:** Apply subtle parallax effects on hero images and gallery blocks to create a sense of depth. Text should fade and drift up gently as it enters the viewport.
* **Custom Cursor (Optional):** A minimalist geometric cursor (perhaps a small dot that expands when hovering over clickable project images) to enhance the bespoke feel.
* **Page Transitions:** Smooth fade-ins and fade-outs between page loads to prevent harsh screen flashes and maintain the "quiet luxury" atmosphere.
