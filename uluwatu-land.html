The Opportunity
Two minutes from Alila and Savaya. A blank canvas for Uluwatu's next landmark.

Just off the road that connects two of Bali's most celebrated resort addresses Alila Villas Uluwatu and Savaya this 20 are parcel sits at the heart of the peninsula's most coveted corridor. With direct main road frontage and a commanding elevation with ocean views, it delivers what very few sites at this level can: genuine accessibility in a location defined by exclusivity.
