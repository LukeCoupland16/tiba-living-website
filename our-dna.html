The Journey to Tiba

Tiba means
"to arrive"

TIBA LIVING is a boutique real estate development company, defined by its authentic approach in design, development, and delivery.

What began as a singular vision on the cliffs of Bali has grown into a global pursuit of one idea: that the places we live should feel inevitable — as if they could exist nowhere else. Every site we choose, every material we specify, every community we build is measured against a single question: does this belong?

From the rice terraces of Pererenan to the ridgelines of Tennessee and the coastlines of California, Tiba Living has remained committed to architecture that earns its landscape rather than conquering it. We are boutique by design — small enough to care deeply, experienced enough to deliver with precision.

Quiet Luxury · Untouched Sanctuaries · Community by Design
Five Principles

What
Drives Us

01

Natural
Assimilation

We study each site — its contours, its materials, its rhythm — before a single line is drawn. The result is architecture that feels as though it grew from the earth itself, borrowing from the land rather than imposing upon it.

02

Sustainable
Development

Responsibility is woven into every decision. From locally sourced timber and volcanic stone to passive cooling systems and rainwater harvesting, we build with the next generation in mind — without compromising the quality that defines Tiba Living.

03

Climate
Adaptability

Every destination has its own atmosphere. Our designs are inherently responsive — embracing the tropical humidity of Bali, the mountain air of the Appalachians, the coastal warmth of California — rather than fighting their environment.

04

Timeless
Development

Trends fade. Our architecture endures. We draw from the vernacular of each region while applying a design intelligence that ages with grace. A Tiba building looks as right in twenty years as it does on the day of completion.

05

Informed
Modernity

We are contemporary without being cold. Each project weaves together the cultural heritage of its location with modern living standards — producing spaces that feel both grounded and forward-thinking, rooted yet reaching.

We do not build what is possible.
We build what is right.

— Tiba Living Philosophy
