The Journey to Tiba

Tiba means
"to arrive"

TIBA LIVING is a boutique real estate development company, defined by its authentic approach in design, development, and delivery.

What began as a singular vision on the cliffs of Bali has grown into a global pursuit of one idea: that the places we live should feel inevitable — as if they could exist nowhere else. Every site we choose, every material we specify, every community we build is measured against a single question: does this belong?

From the cliffs of Bali to the ridgelines of Tennessee, Tiba Living has remained committed to architecture that earns its landscape rather than conquering it. We are boutique by design — small enough to care deeply, experienced enough to deliver with precision.

Quiet Luxury · Untouched Sanctuaries · Community by Design
Five Principles

What
Drives Us

01

Natural Assimilation

We study each site — its contours, its materials, its rhythm — before a single line is drawn. Architecture that feels as though it grew from the earth itself.

02

Sustainable Development

From locally sourced timber and volcanic stone to passive cooling systems and rainwater harvesting, we build with the next generation in mind.

03

Climate Adaptability

Our designs are inherently responsive — embracing the tropical humidity of Bali, the mountain air of the Appalachians, the coastal warmth of California.

04

Timeless Development

Trends fade. Our architecture endures. We draw from the vernacular of each region with a design intelligence that ages with grace.

05

Informed Modernity

Contemporary without being cold. Each project weaves cultural heritage with modern living standards — spaces that feel both grounded and forward-thinking.
