The Brief
Living above the break — where every dawn is framed by the Indian Ocean horizon.

Tiba Lofts was born from a conviction that great architecture should feel inevitable — as though the land itself conceived the form. Perched on the limestone ridgeline of Padang Padang, each residence is oriented to command an unbroken sightline over one of Bali's most celebrated surf breaks, while remaining entirely private from the world below.

The six loft residences are organised as a quiet collection — individual in character, unified in material language. Double-height volumes open to generous terraces, dissolving the threshold between interior and the coastal sky. Locally quarried limestone, weathered timber, and hand-applied plaster compose a palette that will only deepen with the years, each surface marked by the salt air and the passing of seasons.

Design Intent

Height, light,
and open sky.

  • Cliff-Edge Orientation

    Each loft is positioned to frame the surf break and the Indian Ocean horizon — a living view that shifts with the tide and the light of every day.

  • Double-Height Loft Volumes

    Open-plan interiors rise to exposed timber ceilings, drawing the coastal breeze through the space and connecting each room to the sky above.

  • Handcrafted Limestone & Teak

    Sourced from Balinese quarries and milled by local craftsmen, the material palette honours the island's building traditions while embracing a quiet contemporary form.
